# Canon in D

Pachelbel's canon, simplified. The left hand plays the same eight bass
notes over and over; the right hand plays slow notes, then the chords
broken up, one note at a time.

```abc practice: tempo=72 hands=both
X:1
T:Canon in D
C:Pachelbel
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=72
K:D
V:R clef=treble
V:L clef=bass
[V:R] f2 e2 | d2 c2 | B2 A2 | B2 c2 |
[V:L] D,2 A,,2 | B,,2 F,,2 | G,,2 D,,2 | G,,2 A,,2 |
[V:R] d f a f | c e a e | B d f d | A c f c |
[V:L] D,2 A,,2 | B,,2 F,,2 | G,,2 D,,2 | G,,2 A,,2 |
[V:R] G B d B | F A d A | G B d B | A c e c | d4 |]
[V:L] D,2 A,,2 | B,,2 F,,2 | G,,2 D,,2 | G,,2 A,,2 | D,4 |]
```
