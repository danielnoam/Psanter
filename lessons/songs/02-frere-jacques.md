# Frère Jacques, as a round

The left hand plays the same tune an octave lower, two bars behind the
right. Learn the tune with one hand first; then the hard part is keeping
each hand in its own place.

```abc practice: tempo=100 hands=both
X:1
T:Frère Jacques
C:Traditional
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=100
K:C
V:R clef=treble
V:L clef=bass
[V:R] C D E C | C D E C | E F G2 | E F G2 | G/A/G/F/ E C |
[V:L] z4 | z4 | C, D, E, C, | C, D, E, C, | E, F, G,2 |
[V:R] G/A/G/F/ E C | C G, C2 | C G, C2 | E2 G2 | c4 |]
[V:L] E, F, G,2 | G,/A,/G,/F,/ E, C, | G,/A,/G,/F,/ E, C, | C, G,, C,2 | C, G,, C,2 |]
```
