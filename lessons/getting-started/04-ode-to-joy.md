# Ode to Joy

Beethoven's tune sits almost all in C position. Learn the right hand first
with the app playing the left, then swap.

```abc practice: tempo=90 hands=right
X:1
T:Ode to Joy
C:Beethoven
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=90
K:C
V:R clef=treble
V:L clef=bass
[V:R] E E F G | G F E D | C C D E | E3/2 D/ D2 |
[V:L] C,4 | G,,4 | C,4 | G,,2 G,,2 |
[V:R] E E F G | G F E D | C C D E | D3/2 C/ C2 |]
[V:L] C,4 | G,,4 | C,4 | G,,2 C,2 |]
```

The same piece as a MIDI file has no sheet music, only falling notes. Some
pieces you find will be like this:
