# Minuet in G

The opening of the minuet from the Anna Magdalena Bach notebook, now known
to be by Christian Petzold. It's a MusicXML file, the format to use for
real pieces: write or import them in MuseScore and export MusicXML.

[Minuet in G](pieces/minuet-in-g.musicxml "practice tempo=80 hands=right")

Things to watch:

- Bars 1 and 3: the run of eighths stays even. Loop the bar at 60% until
  it is.
- Bar 7: F sharp, the only black key so far.
- The left hand is slow and simple. Once the right hand is clean, play both.
