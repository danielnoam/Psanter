# Loud and soft

How hard you press a key sets how loud it sounds. Music marks it with
letters from Italian: **p** (piano) is soft, **f** (forte) is loud, **mp**
and **mf** are a little soft and a little loud.

After a run, the progress panel shows how well your loud and soft followed
the marks. Your piano's touch is taken into account: it's the difference
between p and f that counts, not the exact level.

```abc practice: tempo=70 hands=right mode=flow
X:1
T:Echoes
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=70
K:C
!f!C E G2 | !p!C E G2 | !f!G F E D | !p!G F E D | !mf!C D E F | !mp!G2 E2 | !f!D2 G2 | !p!C4 |]
```
