# Every key, every day

There are twelve major keys and twelve minor ones. You don't need them all
at once: one key a day goes round all twelve majors in under two weeks.

- **Today's practice** in the library starts with that day's scale.
- **Drills → Scales** writes any of them, one or two octaves, either hand
  or both, with the usual fingering where the key has one.

Here's E minor, which has one sharp (F♯), and in the harmonic form a D♯.
