# Steps in the treble clef

Middle C sits on its own short line below the staff. Each note up goes from
a line to the next space, or a space to the next line: a **step**, one white
key. D hangs under the staff, E is on the bottom line, F in the first space,
G on the second line.

Keep the right hand in C position (thumb on C) and read the direction first:
up, down, or the same.

```abc practice: tempo=70 hands=right
X:1
T:Up and down by step
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=70
K:C
!1!C D E F | G F E D | C D E D | C4 |]
```
