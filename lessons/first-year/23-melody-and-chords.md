# A tune over chords

The right hand plays the tune and the left hand holds the chord under it.
Learn the left hand alone first (set the right hand to the app), then put
them together slowly in wait mode.

```abc practice: tempo=70 hands=both
X:1
T:Mary had a little lamb, with chords
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=70
K:C
V:R clef=treble
V:L clef=bass
[V:R] E D C D | E E E2 | D D D2 | E G G2 | E D C D | E E E E | D D E D | C4 |]
[V:L] [C,E,G,]4 | [C,E,G,]4 | [B,,D,G,]4 | [C,E,G,]4 | [C,E,G,]4 | [C,E,G,]4 | [B,,D,G,]4 | [C,E,G,]4 |]
```
