# D and A major

D major has two sharps (F♯ and C♯), A major three (F♯, C♯ and G♯). Both
keep the C major fingering. Say the sharps out loud before you start.

```abc practice: tempo=60 hands=both
X:1
T:D major, hands together
M:4/4
L:1/8
Q:1/4=60
K:D
V:R clef=treble
V:L clef=bass
[V:R] !1!D !2!E !3!F !1!G !2!A !3!B !4!c !5!d | !4!c !3!B !2!A !1!G !3!F !2!E !1!D2 |]
[V:L] !5!D, !4!E, !3!F, !2!G, !1!A, !3!B, !2!C !1!D | !2!C !3!B, !1!A, !2!G, !3!F, !4!E, !5!D,2 |]
```

```abc practice: tempo=60 hands=both
X:2
T:A major, hands together
M:4/4
L:1/8
Q:1/4=60
K:A
V:R clef=treble
V:L clef=bass
[V:R] !1!A !2!B !3!c !1!d !2!e !3!f !4!g !5!a | !4!g !3!f !2!e !1!d !3!c !2!B !1!A2 |]
[V:L] !5!A,, !4!B,, !3!C, !2!D, !1!E, !3!F, !2!G, !1!A, | !2!G, !3!F, !1!E, !2!D, !3!C, !4!B,, !5!A,,2 |]
```
