# Skips

A **skip** jumps over one note: line to line, or space to space. C to E,
E to G, D to F. On the page a skip looks like two notes on neighbouring
lines (or spaces), and on the keyboard there's one white key between them.

```abc practice: tempo=70 hands=right
X:1
T:Steps and skips
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=70
K:C
!1!C E G E | D F E D | C E D F | E G C2 |]
```

Lightly Row is mostly skips going down and steps going up.

```abc practice: tempo=80 hands=right
X:2
T:Lightly row
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=80
K:C
!5!G E E2 | F D D2 | C D E F | G G G2 | G E E2 | F D D2 | C E G G | E4 |]
```
