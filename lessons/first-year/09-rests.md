# Rests

A rest is a beat of silence, and it's counted just like a note. A quarter
rest is the squiggle, a half rest sits on the middle line like a hat, a
whole rest hangs under the fourth line.

Lift the finger off on each rest, so the silence is real.

```abc practice: tempo=70 hands=right mode=flow metronome=true
X:1
T:Silent beats
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=70
K:C
C z C z | C2 z2 | z C z C | C4 | E z D z | C2 z2 | D z E z | C4 |]
```

Hot cross buns, with a rest at the end of each phrase.

```abc practice: tempo=80 hands=right mode=flow metronome=true
X:2
T:Hot cross buns
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=80
K:C
E D C z | E D C z | C/C/C/C/ D/D/D/D/ | E D C z |]
```
