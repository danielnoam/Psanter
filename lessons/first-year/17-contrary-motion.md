# Contrary motion

Both thumbs start on middle C and the hands move apart, then come back.
The hands mirror each other, so the fingers match: both thumbs tuck under
at the same moment. A good way into hands-together scales.

```abc practice: tempo=60 hands=both loop=true
X:1
T:C major in contrary motion
M:4/4
L:1/8
Q:1/4=60
K:C
V:R clef=treble
V:L clef=bass
[V:R] !1!C !2!D !3!E !1!F !2!G !3!A !4!B !5!c | !4!B !3!A !2!G !1!F !3!E !2!D !1!C2 |]
[V:L] !1!C !2!B, !3!A, !1!G, !2!F, !3!E, !4!D, !5!C, | !4!D, !3!E, !2!F, !1!G, !3!A, !2!B, !1!C2 |]
```
