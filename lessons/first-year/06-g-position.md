# G position

Move the right hand up so the thumb is on G (the second line of the treble
staff) and the fingers cover G, A, B, C and D. The left hand puts its
little finger on the G on the bottom line of the bass staff, and the thumb
on the D in the middle.

```abc practice: tempo=70 hands=right
X:1
T:Right hand in G position
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=70
K:C
!1!G A B c | d c B A | G B A c | B A G2 |]
```

```abc practice: tempo=70 hands=left
X:2
T:Left hand in G position
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=70
K:C clef=bass
!5!G,, A,, B,, C, | D, C, B,, A,, | G,, B,, A,, C, | B,, A,, G,,2 |]
```
