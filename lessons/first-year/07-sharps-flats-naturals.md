# Sharps, flats and naturals

A **sharp** (♯) raises a note to the very next key, usually a black one. A
**flat** (♭) lowers it. A **natural** (♮) cancels either. A sign in front
of a note lasts to the end of the bar.

A **key signature** at the start of each line puts the sharps or flats on
every note of that name. G major has one sharp, F♯: every F is played as
F♯ unless a natural says otherwise.

```abc practice: tempo=70 hands=right
X:1
T:F sharp in G major
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=70
K:G
G A B c | d2 c B | A G F A | G4 |]
```

F major has one flat, B♭.

```abc practice: tempo=70 hands=right
X:2
T:B flat in F major
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=70
K:F
F G A B | c2 B A | G A =B c | F4 |]
```
