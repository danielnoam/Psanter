# Putting it together

Jingle Bells, with the left hand on the roots of the chords: C, F and G.
Practise in pieces: loop four bars at a time (drag on the timeline) until
each is clean, then join them.

```abc practice: tempo=90 hands=both
X:1
T:Jingle bells, both hands
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=90
K:C
V:R clef=treble
V:L clef=bass
[V:R] E E E2 | E E E2 | E G C D | E4 | F F F F | F E E E/E/ | E D D E | D2 G2 | E E E2 | E E E2 | E G C D | E4 | F F F F | F E E E/E/ | G G F D | C4 |]
[V:L] C,4 | C,4 | C,4 | C,4 | F,4 | C,4 | G,4 | G,4 | C,4 | C,4 | C,4 | C,4 | F,4 | C,4 | G,4 | C,4 |]
```
