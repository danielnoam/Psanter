# Minor chords

Lower the middle note of a major triad by a half step and it turns minor:
C–E–G is C major, C–E♭–G is C minor. In A minor the chords are i (A minor),
iv (D minor) and V (E major, with the G♯ from the harmonic minor).

```abc practice: tempo=60 hands=both
X:1
T:i iv V i in A minor
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=60
K:Am
V:R clef=treble
V:L clef=bass
[V:R] [A,CE]2 [A,CE]2 | [A,DF]2 [A,DF]2 | [^G,B,E]2 [^G,B,E]2 | [A,CE]4 |]
[V:L] A,,2 A,,2 | D,2 D,2 | E,2 E,2 | A,,4 |]
```
