# F major

F major has one flat, B♭, and the right hand changes fingering: four
fingers, then the thumb on C, then four again. The thumb never lands on the
black key. The left hand keeps its usual fingering.

```abc practice: tempo=60 hands=right loop=true
X:1
T:F major, right hand
M:4/4
L:1/8
Q:1/4=60
K:F
!1!F !2!G !3!A !4!B !1!c !2!d !3!e !4!f | !3!e !2!d !1!c !4!B !3!A !2!G !1!F2 |]
```

```abc practice: tempo=60 hands=left loop=true
X:2
T:F major, left hand
M:4/4
L:1/8
Q:1/4=60
K:F clef=bass
!5!F,, !4!G,, !3!A,, !2!B,, !1!C, !3!D, !2!E, !1!F, | !2!E, !3!D, !1!C, !2!B,, !3!A,, !4!G,, !5!F,,2 |]
```
