# Eighth notes

Two eighth notes fit in one beat; they're joined by a beam. Count "one and
two and", playing the second eighth on the "and".

```abc practice: tempo=70 hands=right mode=flow metronome=true
X:1
T:One and two and
M:4/4
L:1/8
Q:1/4=70
K:C
C2 C2 C2 C2 | CC CC CC CC | C2 CC C2 CC | C8 |]
```

```abc practice: tempo=80 hands=right mode=flow metronome=true
X:2
T:Running up and down
M:4/4
L:1/8
Q:1/4=80
K:C
CD EF G2 G2 | GF ED C2 C2 | EF GF ED CD | C8 |]
```
