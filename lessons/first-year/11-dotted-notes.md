# Dotted notes and 3/4

A dot adds half again: a dotted half note is three beats, a dotted quarter
a beat and a half. In 3/4 there are three beats in a bar, the first one
strongest: "ONE two three".

```abc practice: tempo=90 hands=right mode=flow metronome=true
X:1
T:A slow waltz
M:3/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=90
K:C
C E G | G3 | F E D | E3 | C E G | c3 | B A G | c3 |]
```

A dotted quarter and an eighth: hold the first note through the "and".

```abc practice: tempo=70 hands=right mode=flow metronome=true
X:2
T:Long, short
M:4/4
L:1/8
Q:1/4=70
K:C
C3 D E2 E2 | D3 E F2 F2 | E3 F G2 E2 | C8 |]
```
