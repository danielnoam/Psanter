# Ties

A **tie** is a curve joining two notes of the same pitch: play the first
and hold it through the second, without playing again. Ties are how a note
lasts across a bar line.

```abc practice: tempo=70 hands=right mode=flow metronome=true
X:1
T:Across the bar
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=70
K:C
C D E F- | F E D C- | C D E2- | E4 | G F E D- | D C D E- | E D C2- | C4 |]
```

With eighths, a tie often holds a note over the middle of the bar or across
it. Count "one and two and" and keep counting through the held notes.

```abc practice: tempo=70 hands=right mode=flow metronome=true
X:2
T:Held over
M:4/4
L:1/8
Q:1/4=70
K:C
C2 D2 E2 F2- | F2 E2 D2 C2 | E2 FG- G2 E2 | C8 |]
```
