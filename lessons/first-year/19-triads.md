# Triads

A **triad** is three notes stacked in skips: a root, the third above it,
and the fifth. On the page it looks like a snowman: three notes on three
lines, or three spaces. Play with fingers 1, 3 and 5, all together.

C, F and G are the three main chords of C major.

```abc practice: tempo=60 hands=right
X:1
T:C, F and G
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=60
K:C
[CEG]4 | [FAc]4 | [GBd]4 | [CEG]4 |]
```

```abc practice: tempo=60 hands=left
X:2
T:The same, left hand
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=60
K:C clef=bass
[C,E,G,]4 | [F,A,C]4 | [G,B,D]4 | [C,E,G,]4 |]
```
