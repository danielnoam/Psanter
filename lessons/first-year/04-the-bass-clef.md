# The bass clef

The left hand reads the bass clef. Its two dots sit either side of the F
line (the fourth line up), which is the F below middle C. The C an octave
below middle C is in the second space.

Left hand in C position: little finger (5) on that C, thumb (1) on G.

```abc practice: tempo=70 hands=left
X:1
T:Left hand by step
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=70
K:C clef=bass
!5!C, D, E, F, | G, F, E, D, | C, E, D, F, | E, D, C,2 |]
```

```abc practice: tempo=70 hands=left
X:2
T:Left hand skips
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=70
K:C clef=bass
!1!G, E, C, E, | G,2 C,2 | D, F, E, G, | C,4 |]
```
