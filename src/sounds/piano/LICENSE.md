# Piano samples

The grand piano sound is the **Salamander Grand Piano** by Alexander Holm,
recorded from a Yamaha C5, released under the Creative Commons Attribution
3.0 licence (CC-BY 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

These 30 MP3s, one every three semitones from A0 to C8, are the ones
prepared by the Tone.js project (https://github.com/Tonejs/audio,
`salamander/`). The app pitches them to the notes between.
